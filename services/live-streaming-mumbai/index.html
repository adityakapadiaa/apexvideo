Live streaming in Mumbai

Mumbai · Over four decades

Service

Multi-camera streams, cut live

Apex Video live streams events in Mumbai and across India with a multi-camera crew and a director cutting between angles in real time, so your online audience watches a finished programme, not a single locked-off camera.

Every stream runs through broadcast-grade video switchers. Camera operators stay connected to the director on wireless talkback, so each cut, close-up and wide is called as it happens.

We have streamed and recorded festivals, concerts and conferences at venues such as the Himalayan Cultural Centre in Dehradun for Virasat, where we are the official videography partner. The same recording is then edited for release, so one shoot gives you both the live broadcast and films to keep.

In the kit · Sony cinematic and broadcast cameras · Broadcast-grade video switchers · Wireless talkback system

Process

How it works

  1. Recce and planWe look at the venue, stage and internet connection, and plan camera positions and the stream setup with you.
  2. Set up and testCameras, switcher, audio feed and talkback are set up and the stream is tested before doors open.
  3. Go liveA director cuts the cameras live while the crew follows the programme on talkback.
  4. DeliverYou receive the full recording, and edited films if you want them.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

Which events can you live stream?

Concerts and music festivals, conferences and conclaves, award shows, talk shows and product launches. If it happens live, we can stream it.

How many cameras do you use?

It depends on the venue and how much of the event you want covered. We plan the camera positions with you after looking at the stage and seating.

Do you stream outside Mumbai?

Yes. We are based in Mumbai and travel for events, most recently to Dehradun for the Virasat festival.

Do we also get a recording?

Yes. The live programme is recorded, and we can edit it into highlight films or individual performances afterwards.
