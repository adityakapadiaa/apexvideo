About us

Thirty years behind the camera.

An event production and multi-camera broadcast team, based in Mumbai.

Dhaval Apex Video is led by Dhaval Kapadia, who has spent more than thirty years behind the camera, filming live events and weddings from Mumbai.

Live events don’t offer a second take, so we work multi-camera, with a director cutting between angles in real time and every operator connected on talkback.

Festival organisers, corporate teams and families come to us for the same reason: they want the whole occasion on record, filmed properly.

FAQ

Questions clients ask first.

What kinds of events do you film?

Concerts and music festivals, conferences and conclaves, award shows, talk shows and product launches, plus weddings and every ceremony around them. If it happens live, we can film it.

Do you travel outside Mumbai?

Yes. We are based in Mumbai and travel for shoots. Recent work includes the Virasat festival in Dehradun.

Can you live stream our event?

Yes. We run multi-camera streams through professional Blackmagic and Datavideo switchers, with the crew connected on wireless talkback so every cut is called live.

What equipment do you use?

Sony Z190 and Z280 broadcast cameras, Blackmagic and Datavideo switchers, and wireless talkback for the camera team. The exact setup depends on your venue and the coverage you need.

What do we receive after a wedding?

That depends on the coverage you choose. As an example, for Yash and Shreya we filmed six ceremonies and delivered highlight films of each celebration.

How do I get a quote?

Send the date, venue and a short brief on WhatsApp, by phone or by email. We will come back with a proposal tailored to your event.
